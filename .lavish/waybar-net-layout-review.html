🛠️ Implementation Specifications gtk.md & Rule Compliant

1. Waybar Script & Module Setup

  • Module location: Replace "hyprland/language" with "custom/net-layout" in config.jsonc lines 4–9.
  • Script directory: ~/.config/waybar/scripts/net-layout/
  • Daemonless: Waybar calls net-layout.py every 1s (interval: 1). Reads /proc/net/dev and stores previous timestamp in memory/temp file.
  • Tooltip: Shows comprehensive stats (Total IP, Gateway, Ping/latency, Session Downloaded/Uploaded, Active Interface).

2. GTK3 Settings Modal (gtk.md)

  • SwayNC Click-Catcher: Anchored to all 4 edges on Layer.TOP with invisible EventBox backdrop.
  • Stateless: Dismiss with Gtk.main_quit() on backdrop click or Escape. No PID files or background daemon.
  • Toggle script: Clean bash launcher with pgrep -f "quick-net-settings.py" and pkill.
  • Matugen theming: Loads colors.css dynamically. No unsupported transform properties.
